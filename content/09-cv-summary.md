## Layout suggestion

- Sidebar on desktop (250–300 px wide), full-width section on mobile
- Or as a single accordion at the bottom of the about page — collapsed by default, opens to reveal the full block
- Make the **GPA / class rank** numbers prominent — they're real signals for French/Indian recruiters
- Date format: "Sept 2025" not "September 2025" — saves space, reads cleaner

## What NOT to put in this block

- Photos
- Quotes / endorsements
- Hobbies (recruiters in Paris food/CPG don't care; if they ask in interview, Baba can answer in person)
- Aspirational job titles (he's a candidate, not a "future CMO")
