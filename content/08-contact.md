## What NOT to put

- Phone number (cold sales)
- Salary expectations
- "I am also available for full-time after May 2026" if you're targeting stage — pick one signal, not both
- A contact form (forms get spammed; mailto + LinkedIn is enough)
- Captcha / reCAPTCHA (kills conversion; recruiters bounce)
- "Reply within 24 hours guaranteed" (overpromising; ghosting recruiters destroys reputation faster than slow replies)
