# Case study #3 — IMPACT Consulting, brand strategy work

**Section title on site:** "Brand-side, client-facing"
**Subtitle:** IMPACT Consulting, Hyderabad · Sept 2023 – Mar 2024 · Project Support Intern

The shortest case study, but the only one with explicit **brand and consumer-facing work**. Lead with this when the role is **brand management, consumer insights, or anything where the recruiter wants to see hands-on brand experience**.

---

## Hero metric (big, centered)

> **5 business units · -40% reporting time · brand strategy meets analytics**

## Situation

IMPACT Consulting is a Hyderabad-based marketing and brand-strategy consultancy serving Indian and South Asian clients across multiple categories. I joined as a Project Support Intern in September 2023, after my four years at Srikanth and before starting the Master in Management at ESSEC.

The 6-month engagement was structured around two parallel tracks: **client-facing brand work** and **internal analytics tooling** — a deliberate combination that put me on the brand side without leaving the dashboard rigour I'd built at Srikanth.

## Task

Two mandates:

1. **Support brand-strategy projects** across **5 client business units**, in a client-facing capacity — exposure to consumer positioning, campaign planning, and brand communication.
2. **Build analytics tooling** to make the consultancy's own recruitment funnel measurable.

## Action

### Brand-side track

- Sat in on consumer-positioning workshops across 5 BUs — saw the conversation move from "we sell X" to "we make Y feel Z to a person who wants W."
- Contributed to **campaign planning** documents — translation between client briefs and creative direction.
- Helped draft **brand-communication frameworks** (tone of voice, visual hierarchy, channel mix).

### Analytics track

- Built **Power BI recruitment-analytics dashboards** to monitor funnel performance and conversion metrics.
- Standardised the data model so the dashboard could refresh itself weekly without manual work.

## Result

- **40% reduction in reporting time** for the consultancy's recruitment funnel — what used to take a partner half a day every Monday now took 20 minutes of dashboard review.
- **First-hand exposure to brand work across 5 BUs** — the moment when I knew I wanted to do this side of the business as a career, not just operations.

## Reflection — what I learned

> *"Brand is downstream of consumer judgment. The dashboard tells you what people did; the brand workshop tells you why — and only the second one tells you what to do next."*

Two specific lessons:

1. **The dashboard cannot replace the brand conversation.** I came into IMPACT thinking analytics would be the answer to most questions; I left realising that the analytics tells you the past, but the brand workshop is where you decide the future.
2. **Career pivots happen in 6-month engagements, not in 4-year jobs.** Six months at IMPACT is what put me on the marketing path. Without it, I'd still be doing operations.

## Why this transfers

This is the case study to lead with for **brand manager, brand strategy, agency-side, consultancy consumer practice, or any "show me you've held a client brief"** role. It's also the place to bridge the operations-PM background to the marketing pivot — IMPACT is the inflection point.
